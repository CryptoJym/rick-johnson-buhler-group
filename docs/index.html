Conclusions and recommendation

What we think this means, and why the deck asks for a meeting.

The data supports a focused AI-answer visibility baseline. It does not support a broad scare-based SEO pitch or a formal score claim yet.

Conclusion 1: strong known-brand retrieval

Named product and brand-assisted queries are working. That is why the deck does not position Buhler as invisible or failing.

Conclusion 2: broad discovery is uneven

Category, service, digital, lifecycle, and some regional narratives are less consistently captured. That is the real opportunity.

Conclusion 3: service architecture matters

Research says service and digital lifecycle support are strategic. Search results do not yet make that story reliably easy to retrieve from broad prompts.

Recommendation: validate, then baseline

Use the deck to confirm priorities with Buhler, then run 20-30 validated prompts and return a prioritized source/citation fix plan.

What to verify with Buhler

Confirm the correct North America lens, the most valuable category priorities, Mexico manufacturing timing/status, official source ownership, and whether service/digital/lifecycle positioning should lead the first pilot.
